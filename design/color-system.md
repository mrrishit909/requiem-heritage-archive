# Color system: archive and ruin

| Token | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Charcoal | #111111 | Page, canvas clear colour, fog, the iris's outside |
| Stone | #A49E91 | Stone parts, secondary text |
| Parchment | #E9DFC8 | Text, plaster, the reconstruction layer, the damage ramp's low end |
| Oxide | #945D43 | Timber, tile, type labels, the middle of the damage ramp |
| Dust | #CFC6B4 | Courtyard, drifting dust, body text |
| Archive Red | #8A2424 | The top of the damage ramp, the timeline's event ticks, archive anchors |

The damage layer runs parchment (sound) to oxide to archive red (lost), so the same palette that dresses the museum reads as a condition survey on the building.

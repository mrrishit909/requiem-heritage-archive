# Motion spec

- The date scrubber physically restores and erodes geometry: a part is built in over 4 years and crumbles over 1.5 years after its lost year, by noise threshold in the fragment shader plus a vertex fall, with fragments left as debris on the ground.
- Archive media emerge from anchors: a red ring on the building, a leader line, and a card that scales out from the anchor to its place.
- Rooms connect by arch: the iris closing and opening in the shape of the gate arch is used on the way into the structure and on the way out.
- Dust reacts to movement: the dust field drifts opposite to the camera's velocity and settles.
- Text is editorial and restrained: serif, tracked capitals for the statement, no bounce.
- Panels turn by clip-path from the top edge. Reduced motion removes the iris, the panel turn and the ambient motion.
